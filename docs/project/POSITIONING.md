# Positioning

EnergyHub helps a supported home use solar wisely while preparing for outages.
It connects battery/inverter evidence, forecasts, warnings, schedules and
selected household devices through one local policy and understandable Telegram
reports. Economy when conditions allow; preparedness when conditions demand it.

The current snapshot is a commissioned monolithic reference implementation,
not a universal commercial Core. Its value is coordination and explainable
decisions—not a claim to prevent external outages or guarantee savings.
Local control still depends on external providers for forecasts/messages.

Native HA plug protection, EH-owned HP1/inverter management and Family reporting
have distinct authority/confirmation boundaries. Diagrams explain these honestly.
Future Core/Custom delivery aims to make installation differences configurable;
AI should request validated intents rather than replace deterministic safety.

EV charging, multi-slot tariff optimization and export/Net Billing need separate
hardware, contract and policy validation. They are plans, not current features.
No competitor-superiority or measured-savings claim is established here.
