  60% sustained recovery; sequential actions with persisted ownership.
- First-floor HP native Heat/Off precedes second/third-floor relay control.

- Short Ukrainian confirmed-action messages; no duplicate completion message.

  against the family's current setting. Compact morning and battery-jump wording.
- Keep entity IDs/history and Threat Monitor unchanged.

See [control contract and limitations](docs/design/PEAK_LOAD_CONTROL.md) and
validation / attended deployment gates (private development record).
